QR Scanner – Privacy Policy

App: QR Scanner (com.kobimalaki.qrscanner)
Developer: Kobi Malaki
Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy explains how the QR Scanner app handles information. In short: everything stays on your phone. The developer does not collect, receive, or share your personal data.

Information we collect

QR Scanner has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, and no advertising. The app does not send any of your data to the developer or to any server operated by the developer.

Camera

The app asks for permission to use your camera so it can read QR codes. Camera images are processed on your device in real time to detect a code. They are not saved, recorded, or sent anywhere.

Photos you choose

If you use the option to scan a QR code from a photo, the app reads only the single image you select, on your device. It does not access the rest of your photos or upload the image.

Scan history and favorites

When you scan a code, the app can save the result in a history list stored only on your phone. You can mark items as favorites, search them, and delete them one by one. In Settings you can turn off history saving, and you can clear your history at any time (favorites are kept unless you delete them). Uninstalling the app removes all saved history. This data is never uploaded or shared by the app.

Creating QR codes

QR codes you create are generated on your device from the text you type. If you choose Save image, the picture is stored in the Pictures/QR Scanner folder on your phone. If you choose Share image, the app passes the picture to the app you select.

Settings

Your choices, such as vibration, beep, history saving, and opening links automatically, are stored only on your phone.

Actions you choose

Nothing happens with a scan result unless you choose to act on it:

  • Copy places the text on your device clipboard.
  • Share sends the text or image to the app you pick.
  • Open launches another app on your phone, such as your browser, phone dialer, email app, or maps, to handle the link or content.

The Rate this app and Follow on YouTube items open the Google Play Store and YouTube. Websites and apps you open are governed by their own privacy policies. Only open links from sources you trust.

Third-party services

The app uses Google ML Kit barcode scanning, delivered through Google Play services, to detect QR codes. Detection runs on your device. Google Play services may collect limited technical or usage information under Google's own privacy policy, available at policies.google.com/privacy.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
